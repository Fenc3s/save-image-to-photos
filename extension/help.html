MADE FOR macOS

Save Image to Photos

Right-click. Share. Add to Photos.

1 Right-click an image and choose Save Image to Photos.

2 Choose Add to Photos in the macOS share sheet.

The extension prepares the selected image on the current page and tries to open system sharing directly. No preparation tab or companion app is required.

After the first use on a page, later image right-clicks on that same page can prepare the image while its menu is open. This may load the right-clicked image even if you choose another action. Leaving the page stops this preparation. See Privacy for details.

Some image hosts require an optional site permission. If Chrome needs a fresh click after a slow download, a small panel on the original page offers Open Share Sheet. No automatic Photos selection is available.

Use public HTTPS JPEG, PNG, GIF, or WebP images up to 20 MiB. WebP is converted to a still PNG. Site sharing policies, cross-origin frames, browser pages, login-only images, and redirects may prevent sharing. Open the image in its own HTTPS tab when a page blocks sharing.
